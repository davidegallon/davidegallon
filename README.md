# Davide Gallon

I am a PhD researcher in Applied Mathematics at the University of Münster. I work on diffusion models for scientific computing, neural methods for PDEs, and stochastic optimization.

## Selected research code

### PISD · ICML 2026 · First author

I independently wrote the PyTorch implementation for PISD, a spectral diffusion method for generating PDE solutions and reconstructing them from sparse observations. The release includes spectral preprocessing, distributed training with `torchrun`, inference configurations, and pretrained checkpoints.

[Code](https://github.com/deeplearningmethods/PISD) · [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/gallon26a.html)

### INEUS · NeurIPS 2026 · Coauthor

I reorganized and polished the public research code and independently implemented the 100-dimensional nonlinear Black–Scholes experiment with default risk, with and without jumps.

[Code](https://github.com/jdupret97/INEUS) · [Experiment guide](https://github.com/jdupret97/INEUS/blob/main/docs/nonlinear_black_scholes.md) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06281)

### SAD Neural Networks · NeurIPS 2025 · Coauthor

I wrote and maintain the experimental software, including training and evaluation for polynomial regression, the heat and Black–Scholes PDEs, and MNIST classification.

[Code](https://github.com/deeplearningmethods/sad) · [Paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/05fb0f4e645cad23e0ab59d6b9901428-Abstract-Conference.html)
